Working with industry

Partnering with SteadyBeat Technology

In September 2026, Fei Tian College Northern Campus signed a memorandum of understanding with SteadyBeat Technology, a Taiwanese company working on AI and autonomous unmanned vehicles. Together we are exploring research and development on campus, and workforce development for students: short courses, workshops, industry talks, student projects, internships, and mentoring.

The agreement is a framework for working together. Specific programs and projects are still being planned.
