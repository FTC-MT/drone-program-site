Train for competition day

This course gets you ready for our drone competition. Pilot real drone soccer drones, caged and built tough for beginners, and build the speed and precision you'll need for the timed contest on our campus. Along the way, learn how drones use physics, sensors, and control systems to fly, and tour our drone research lab.

Open to high schoolers and adults. Beginners and experienced fliers welcome!

High school students

A fun, hands-on way to get into drones and STEM, with a certificate to show for it and a real competition to train for.

Adults, through continuing education

Whether you're brand new or already fly, sharpen your piloting for the competition and earn your FAA recreational certificate.

What you'll learn

Six hands-on sessions take you from your first takeoff to ready for competition day.

  1. 1. Safety and the rules

    Learn the rules of the sky and pass the FAA TRUST test in class.

  2. 2. Drone basics

    Controls, pre-flight checks, and simulator practice.

  3. 3. Real flying

    Takeoff, steady hovering, and soft landings.

  4. 4. Control

    Up and down, side to side, and flight patterns.

  5. 5. Precision

    Flying through hoops, touch-and-go landings, and target hits.

  6. 6. Competition prep

    Timed practice runs on the contest course.

The sky's the limit!

Every participant gets a tour of Fei Tian's drone research lab.

Love coding or engineering? Fei Tian students can join the lab's work on flight simulation and software, and exceptionally talented participants, high schoolers and adults alike, will be considered for internships in a lab with real industry connections.
