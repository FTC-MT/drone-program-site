Learn to fly. Then compete.

A short course on the science, systems, and rules of drone flight, built around the FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot test. Then put your skills to work in our drone contest on campus, where the top three pilots win Fei Tian College scholarships.

Open to high school and college students. No experience needed, just bring your ambition.

High school students, grades 10–12

From schools within driving distance of Middletown. Get a head start in aviation and STEM, and a shot at a college scholarship.

First- and second-year college students

From community colleges and other schools nearby. Build real drone knowledge and prepare for your FAA remote pilot certificate.

The course at a glance

Five 2-hour sessions, mostly online, with one or two in person on campus.

  1. 1. How drones fly

    Lift, drag, and thrust, and the controls that steer a drone.

  2. 2. Drone systems and safety

    The parts of a drone system, remote control, maneuvers, and flying safely.

  3. 3. Weather and airspace

    How the atmosphere affects flight, and where you're allowed to fly.

  4. 4. FAA test practice

    Work through Part 107 practice questions.

  5. 5. Test sign-up and review

    Register for your Part 107 test and review the whole course.

  6. Then: contest day

    Three flying challenges, three tries each pilot, and scholarships for the top three.

Full course and contest details

The contest

Fly quadcopters we provide in a 20 × 20 × 10 ft flight space. Every pilot gets three tries, and the best score counts.

Speed delivery

Race a delivery route against the clock, touching down on each landing pad along the way.

Precision corridor

Thread your drone through a set corridor and hoop with skill and control.

Endurance loop

Fly a smooth, repeating loop pattern for as long as you can.

Prizes: Fei Tian College scholarships for the top three: a full scholarship for first place, $9,000 a year in tuition for second, and $5,000 a year for third. Every participant is invited to a celebration reception.

Your future takes flight!

Every participant gets a tour of Fei Tian's drone research lab.

Love coding or engineering? Fei Tian students can join the lab's work on flight simulation and software, and exceptionally talented participants, high school and college students alike, will be considered for internships in a lab with real industry connections.
